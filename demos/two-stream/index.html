Two-Stream Instability

A one-dimensional electrostatic particle-in-cell code, running live. Two electron beams stream through each other; the plasma wave between them grows until the beams trap and roll into vortices.

What this is solving

Charge is deposited from the particles onto the grid with a cloud-in-cell shape, the longitudinal field follows by integrating dE/dx = ρ along the box and removing the mean, and the particles are advanced by leapfrog. Units are normalized so that the plasma frequency of the whole population is one.

Two counter-streaming beams are unstable for k v₀ < √2 ω_p. The growth rate plotted alongside the measurement is the unstable root of 1 = (ω_p²/2)[1/(ω − k v₀)² + 1/(ω + k v₀)²] at the box's fundamental mode, solved exactly rather than fitted. The measurement is a running fit to the mode amplitude and only becomes meaningful after the seeded perturbation settles onto the growing branch, which is why it is blank at first and drifts once the beams trap and growth stops.

The same dispersion relation, the same deposition and the same push are what the Python package runs; this is the browser-sized version of the benchmark in tests/unit/test_plasma_scenarios.py.
