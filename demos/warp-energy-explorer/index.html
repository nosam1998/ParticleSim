Warp energy-density explorer

The Alcubierre warp bubble's Eulerian energy density ρ = −(v²/32π) · (y²+z²)/rs² · f′(rs)², on the z = 0 plane at t = 0, with the top-hat shape function f(r) = [tanh(σ(r+R)) − tanh(σ(r−R))] / (2 tanh(σR)) in geometric units (G = c = 1). The bubble moves along x; ρ vanishes inside and outside it and is negative everywhere in the wall (the red half of the colour scale never lights up), which is the energy-condition violation that makes warp drives exotic. Everything is computed in your browser from the closed form, with no Python and no precomputed data. This is the first static browser demo from Section 5.7 of the design document; particlesim run examples/configs/warp_alcubierre.yaml (Mode W1, Section 3.2) gives the same numbers.
