ParticleSim demos

Static browser demos from the ParticleSim research simulation framework, as described in Section 5.7 of the design document. No backend: everything on these pages runs in your browser.

  • Warp energy-density explorer
    Alcubierre warp bubble: sliders for velocity, radius and wall thickness over a live heatmap of the analytic Eulerian energy density.
  • Warp bubble view
    What a passenger inside an Alcubierre bubble sees: light traced back through the bubble to the sky on your GPU with WebGPU (or the CPU without it), tinted by its exact frequency shift 1 − v cosα. Above light speed, the sky behind goes dark.
  • Friedmann integrator
    The two background solvers ParticleSim runs in Python, in the page: a ΛCDM budget integrated for ages and distances, and a theory plugin's own H²(ρ) integrated through a crunch under general relativity or a bounce under effective loop quantum cosmology.
  • Two-stream instability
    A one-dimensional electrostatic particle-in-cell code running live in the page. Two electron beams stream through each other, the plasma wave between them grows, and the measured growth rate is plotted against the exact root of the dispersion relation.
